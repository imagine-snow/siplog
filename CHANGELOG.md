# Changelog

## 0.5.3 — 2026-10-27

First beta release of SipLog.

- Guided drink logging with bottle sizes, alcohol content and estimated consumption.
- Bottle inventory, personal and shared consumption, and an editable journal.
- Calendar views and summaries of drinking volume and spending.
- Searchable drink catalog with documented sources and manual entry.
- Chinese and English interfaces.
- Local photo covers, backup and restore, and two-step data reset confirmation.

See [README.md](README.md) for setup and [DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md) for catalog references.
